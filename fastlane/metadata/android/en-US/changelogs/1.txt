First Husk release.

Husk forks Olauncher and adds: a dialer with contact and call log search, home screen media controls, a notification line and panel, per-app internet blocking through a local VPN, a Substack reading list, a two-column home grid, background options, and full Arabic support in a matching pixel typeface.

Removed from upstream: daily wallpaper rotation, which fetched an index from the upstream author's personal gist, and the Play Store rating prompt.

Known limits are listed in the README. The launcher icon is a placeholder, and translations other than English and Arabic are inherited and stale.
